Lyne ADAS

Privacy Policy

Lyne ADAS for Android · Effective 25 November 2026

In short: Lyne runs entirely on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Nothing is collected by us or shared with anyone.

What Lyne uses and why

DataWhyWhere it goes
CameraTo see the road and detect vehicles, pedestrians, riders, animals, lanes and signs.Frames are processed in memory on your phone and discarded. They are stored only if you turn on the event dashcam (below).
Location (precise and approximate)To read your speed from GPS, so warnings are timed correctly and oncoming or passing traffic is not mistaken for a hazard.Used on your phone. A drive's route is saved in your trip history on the phone.

Lyne does not use the microphone, contacts, photos or any account, and does not request internet access.

What is stored on your phone

  • Trip history: start time, duration, distance, top speed, the warnings you received and, if location was on, the route. Stored in the app's private storage.
  • Dashcam clips (only if you turn on the event dashcam): short video clips without sound around critical warnings, in the app's own folder. The 40 most recent are kept and older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Settings and calibration: your preferences, vehicle type and learned camera calibration.
  • Diagnostics (developer options, off by default): accuracy logs, warning logs and crash reports.

All of this stays on your phone. It leaves only if you choose to share a clip, log or report yourself using Android's share menu, and then only to the app or person you pick.

Uninstalling Lyne, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Sharing and selling

We do not collect, sell, rent or share any personal data. There are no third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics. Android's location service (Google Play services) provides the GPS fix to the app on your device; its handling is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Your choices and control

  • Permissions: you can allow or deny camera, location, Bluetooth and notifications, and change them any time in Android settings. Without the camera Lyne cannot work; without location it works with fewer warnings.
  • Automatic clean-up: Lyne keeps only your 50 most recent trips and 40 most recent dashcam clips; older ones are deleted automatically. You can turn the dashcam off in Settings at any time.
  • Delete everything: uninstall Lyne, or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Access and correction: because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give you, correct or erase. Everything Lyne stores is on your phone and under your control.

Security

Trip history, settings and diagnostics are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Dashcam clips are kept in the app's own folder. Because nothing is sent over the internet, there is no server or transfer that could be breached.

Children

Lyne is a driving aid intended for licensed adult drivers and is not directed at children.

Terms of use

Your use of Lyne is also covered by the Terms of Use, including the safety notice that Lyne is a warning-only aid and you remain responsible for driving.
