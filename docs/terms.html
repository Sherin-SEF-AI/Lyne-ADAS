1. What Lyne is

Lyne uses your phone's camera, GPS and motion sensors to watch the road ahead and give sound and on-screen warnings, such as a possible forward collision, a pedestrian, rider or animal in your path, following too closely, leaving your lane, or a speed limit. All processing happens on your phone.

Lyne does not control your vehicle. It is not a replacement for your attention, your mirrors, your vehicle's own safety systems, or safe driving.

2. Limits you must understand

  • Lyne is not a certified or approved automotive safety system under any national or international standard.
  • Detection is based on a camera and machine-learning models. It can miss vehicles, people, animals, lane markings or signs, warn late, or warn when there is no danger.
  • Accuracy drops at night, in rain, fog, dust or glare, on a dirty windscreen, on poorly marked roads, with an unusual mounting angle, when the phone is hot and slows down, and when GPS speed is unavailable.
  • Distances, speeds and timings shown in the app are estimates, not measurements.
  • Never rely on Lyne to decide when to brake, steer, change lanes or overtake. The absence of a warning does not mean it is safe.

3. Your responsibilities

  • You must be at least 18 years old and hold a valid licence for the vehicle you are driving.
  • Keep your eyes on the road and obey all traffic laws. Set the app up before you start driving and do not handle the phone while the vehicle is moving.
